**White Belts only.** Pick one primary path for Season 1 (you may add others later).

| Command | Track | Notebooks (repo) |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| `!track c1` | AlphaZero → MuZero | `1.TicTacToe` … `16.MuZeroAtariTrain` |
| `!track c2` | LLM → DeepSeek | `llm-c-from-scratch/1.Tokens` … `18.DeepSeekV4Path` |

## After choosing

1. Open the first lesson forum for your track
2. Run the matching gate: `dojo-grade --lesson C1-L01` (or `C2-L01`)
3. Paste the `PASS-*` line — bot grants `@C1-L01` etc.

*Wrong tier for LLM? Check `#verify-setup` — you may need better RAM or a cloud GPU.*
